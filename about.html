PitStop is built for quick local decisions around Maple Ridge and nearby BC communities.

Why PitStop?

When you need a washroom, refill point, charger or parking, PitStop puts useful mapped amenities into one focused interface.

Fast by design

A local embedded fallback appears immediately. Live OpenStreetMap data is refreshed after the first render instead of blocking the map.

Local-first

The experience starts around Maple Ridge, then expands through live map data as your radius changes.

Transparent data

Fallback records are labeled as community fallback information, while live records identify OpenStreetMap as their source. Neither means PitStop guarantees current on-site conditions.
